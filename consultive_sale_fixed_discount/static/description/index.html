Fixed Amount Discount

Apply precise, fixed-value discounts directly on Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, and Invoices/Bills without doing manual percentage math.

✔ Sales ✔ Purchases ✔ Invoicing

✨ Core Features

💰 Fixed Amount Input

Enter a flat discount amount (e.g., $50) instead of a percentage. The module automatically reverse-calculates and applies the exact standard percentage needed to reach your total.

🔄 Bi-directional Sync

Change the fixed amount, and the percentage updates. Change the percentage, and the fixed amount updates. Always perfectly in sync.

🛠 Unified Workflow

Discounts flow seamlessly from Sales Orders to Customer Invoices, and from Purchase Orders to Vendor Bills without dropping any data.

⚠ Important: Access Rights & Edge Cases

To prevent confusion for implementers and users, please note the following security behaviors tied to standard Odoo access rights:

  • Sales Orders: The Fixed Discount field on Sales Orders is protected by the sale.group_discount_per_so_line access group. If a user cannot see the standard percentage discount, they cannot see the fixed discount.
  • Purchase Orders: The Fixed Discount field on Purchase Orders and Vendor Bills does not require a specific access group in standard Odoo, but it may be hidden by default under the optional columns (three-dots icon).
  • Accounting Transfers: The fixed discount calculation is passed implicitly when generating Invoices or Bills. Any adjustment on the invoice line will re-trigger the sync between fixed and percentage discounts.
  • Rounding Exceptions: Because Odoo core operates on percentages for pricing, the fixed amount uses a high-precision percentage behind the scenes to avoid rounding errors (syncs if difference is > 0.0001).

📖 Quick Start Guide

1. Enable Standard Discounts (Crucial Step)

Because this module builds on Odoo's core discount logic, you must enable discounts in the base apps:

  • Sales: Go to Sales > Configuration > Settings and check Discounts.
  • Purchases: Go to Purchase > Configuration > Settings and check Purchase Discounts (if available in your Odoo version).

2. Applying on Sales & Purchase Orders

  1. Open a Sale Order or Purchase Order and add a product to the line.
  2. Look for the Fixed Discount column. (Note: If it's hidden, click the 'optional columns' icon (slider/three-dots) on the far right of the list headers and check 'Fixed Discount').
  3. Type a flat value (e.g., 50.00). The standard percentage discount column will calculate automatically based on the unit price and quantity.
  4. When you confirm the order and Create Invoice / Create Bill, the fixed discount seamlessly carries over to the accounting entries.

3. Standalone Invoices & Bills

You do not have to start from an order! You can use this directly in the Accounting app:

  1. Go to Accounting > Customers > Invoices or Accounting > Vendors > Bills.
  2. Create a new record and add an invoice line.
  3. Enter the Fixed Discount directly on the invoice line. The percentage and totals will recalculate instantly.
